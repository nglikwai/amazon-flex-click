Features

Everything you need to
win the refresh game

Flex Slotter is purpose-built for speed, accuracy, and control — so you only grab slots worth your time.

⚡

Lightning-fast refresh

Configurable down to 1.5 s. Flex Slotter reacts the moment a slot appears — before any human can.

💰

Smart earnings filter

Set a minimum and maximum earnings threshold. Only slots within your range will be grabbed.

⏱

Hourly rate filter

OCR reads the working hours from the screen and computes the average $/hr. Skip slots that don't pay enough per hour.

📸

Auto screenshot

Every scan saves a debug screenshot next to your config file, so you can diagnose OCR issues at any time.

✉️

Email notification

Get an instant email with a screenshot of the grabbed slot the moment Flex Slotter books a delivery block for you.

🎯

Pixel-perfect targeting

Pick capture areas and button positions with a visual picker. No config files, no guessing coordinates.

How it works

Set it and forget it

Configure once, then let Flex Slotter run in the background while you do anything else.

01

Configure your targets

Use the visual picker to mark the earnings region, time region, and button positions on your screen. Set your minimum earnings and hourly rate.

02

Hit Start

Flex Slotter begins refreshing the Amazon Flex app at your chosen interval, scanning for new slots via OCR.

03

Slot detected & grabbed

When a matching slot appears, the bot clicks through and books it — faster than any human can react.

04

You get notified

An email lands in your inbox with the earnings details and a screenshot of the booked slot. Bot stops automatically.
