# Quick Setup for New Mac

## Two Simple Steps:

### 1️⃣ Remove "App is Damaged" Error
```bash
xattr -cr /Applications/Amazon\ Flex\ Slotter.app
```

### 2️⃣ Grant Permissions
1. Open **System Settings** → **Privacy & Security**
2. Add app to **Accessibility** (click lock 🔒, enter password, click ➕, select app)
3. Add app to **Screen Recording**
4. **Restart the app**

---

## Why It Can't Click

The app needs **Accessibility permission** to control the mouse. Without this, it cannot automate clicks.

**How to fix:**
- System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility
- Add Amazon Flex Slotter
- ✅ Enable it
- Restart the app
